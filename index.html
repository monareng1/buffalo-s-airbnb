Self-catering 1 Bedroom Apartment • Greenpark, Boksburg

Welcome to Your Home Away From Home

Buffalo's BnB is a self-catering 1 bedroom apartment located in Greenpark, Boksburg. Situated just 10 minutes from O.R. Tambo International Airport, our goal is to give every guest a true home-away-from-home experience.

Founded in 2021 by Ray and Elizabeth Monareng, Buffalo's BnB is recognized for exceptional hospitality, affordable quality accommodation, and creating a welcoming, safe environment for every visitor.
