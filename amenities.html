Apartment Amenities & Location Highlights

Everything you need for a comfortable stay inside Greenpark Eco-Estate and nearby.

Apartment Features

• Self-Catering Kitchen: Fully equipped with modern appliances, microwave, stove, and cookware.

• Entertainment & Connectivity: High-speed uncapped Wi-Fi and Smart TV for continuous streaming.

• Comfort Bedding: Plush queen-size bed with premium linens for a restful sleep.

• Outdoor Space: Private balcony with relaxing seating and scenic estate views.

Greenpark Estate Facilities

• 24/7 Security: Gated access control, biometric entry, and continuous security patrols.

• Leisure Pool: Access to the resort-style swimming pool and splash zone.

• Outdoor Social Hub: Communal braai/barbecue areas, landscaped gardens, and outdoor chess.

Location & Proximity

• O.R. Tambo Airport: Situated just 10 minutes away, ideal for flight crews and layover guests.

• Gautrain Access: Quick access to Rhodesfield Gautrain Station connecting to Sandton and Pretoria.

• Estate Setting: Nestled in Greenpark, Boksburg—a serene, secure residential environment in East Rand.

Surrounding Attractions

• East Rand Mall: Major regional shopping center located minutes away, offering extensive retail, dining, and cinema.

• Dining & Nightlife: Close proximity to local restaurants, cafes, and Emperors Palace Entertainment Resort.

• Convenience: Quick access to local grocery stores, pharmacies, and major highway interchanges (N12/R21).

Prime Location: Enjoy quiet, secure estate living without losing quick access to Johannesburg's major transit routes and shopping hubs!
