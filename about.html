About Buffalo's BnB

Our story, history, and commitment to hospitality.

Brief History

Founded in 2021 by husband and wife Ray Monareng and Elizabeth Monareng, Buffalo's BnB began with a simple shared passion: to redefine local lodging into a genuine home-away-from-home experience. Nestled in the peaceful, secure precinct of Greenpark, Boksburg—just a brief 10-minute drive from O.R. Tambo International Airport—our self-catering 1 bedroom apartment was crafted specifically to bridge the gap between premium hospitality and true affordability.

Over the years, Ray and Elizabeth have cultivated a sanctuary tailored for short-stay travelers, flight crews, business professionals, and holidaymakers alike. By combining 24/7 estate security, immaculate interior furnishings, and attentive, personal hospitality, Buffalo's BnB has quickly earned a reputation for excellence across Johannesburg. Whether you are arriving from a long international flight or seeking a quiet retreat in Gauteng, our commitment remains absolute: ensuring every single guest enjoys a safe, relaxing, and memorable home away from home.

Vision Statement (Worldwide Preference)

To be the favorite and preferred BnB worldwide for when visiting South Africa and near the airport or even just for a holiday recognized for exceptional hospitality, affordable quality accommodation, and creating a welcoming home-away-from-home experience for every guest.

Vision Statement (Guest Commitment)

To provide exceptional, affordable accommodation with warm hospitality, ensuring every guest enjoys a safe, comfortable and memorable home away from home experience in Greenpark, Johannesburg.

"Giving people a warm, safe, and memorable home away from home." — Founders Ray & Elizabeth Monareng
